Independent software

Small tools, made with care.

XN Software makes tools that live on your own machine: an agent that does the work, a watch that catches the idea, an editor that makes it move, and an island that shows it all happening.

Meet the four of them

01 Agent · macOS, Linux and Windows

Flash

An agent for your terminal and your browser, running on models you choose, on your own machine.

Flash reads your code, edits it a line at a time, runs your commands, drives a real browser and talks out loud. It asks before it acts, and checks its own work before it calls anything done.

  • No account and no API keys. It runs on Ollama, on this computer or another of yours.
  • A team of agents that keeps working while you sleep, and reports back in the morning.
  • Courses, chess lessons, a daily journal and your smart home, all in the same chat.

02 Apple Watch

Ember

Raise your wrist, say the idea, and it's kept.

Ember saves every idea on the watch first, then hands it to your computer over Bluetooth when you're near. No iPhone needed, no Wi-Fi, and nothing leaves your desk.

  • One tap from the app, a complication, Siri or the Action Button.
  • Voice notes are transcribed on your own computer, and come back to your wrist.
  • Flash can search your ideas, add to them and act on them.

03 Motion graphics · In the browser

Riptide

A motion graphics and image editor that opens in a browser tab.

Keyframe anything with 23 easing curves, cut video and audio on a real timeline, and work in 3D with lit shapes, imported models and a camera. Then export MP4, WebM, GIF or PNG.

  • 44 animation presets, dropped straight onto a layer.
  • Image mode for quick edits, looks and exports.
  • Flash sends its pictures and 3D models in, live, as new layers.
Watch the video View on GitHub

04 macOS and Windows

Isola

A Dynamic Island for your Mac or PC. Free, and open source.

Music, widgets, Live Activities, your files and your clipboard, one glance away at the top of your screen. On a MacBook it grows out of the notch; on any other screen it floats.

  • 25 live widgets, arranged right in the island.
  • Drop files in, or shake one to zip, convert or share it.
  • Watch Flash work: its plan ticks off beside the notch.
